        Storage rules

        • Data is stored in a SQLite database on the server, separately for each account.
        • Timestamps are stored in UTC and shown in Korea time (KST, UTC+9). Dates are Korea calendar dates.
        • All durations are in minutes.
        • Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes. You stay logged in for 7 days unless you log out.
